# Support

Kyntex is an early-stage engineering project and does not provide medical,
clinical, or emergency support.

For reproducible software defects, open an issue in the relevant repository and
use its bug-report template. For general project context, begin with the
[Kyntex overview](https://github.com/Kyntex-org/Kyntex).

For private support or privacy questions, email
[kyntexsupport@gmail.com](mailto:kyntexsupport@gmail.com). Do not include
passwords, private recordings, health information, or security-sensitive
device details in email.

Do not post credentials, private recordings, health information, or
security-sensitive device details. Report security concerns according to
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
